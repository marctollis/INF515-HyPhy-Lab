# Canvas quiz — Gene, branch, and codon evidence

Complete this quiz in Canvas after working through BUSTED, aBSREL, and MEME. This quiz is the sole submission: no response document, run log, tree image, JSON files, spreadsheet, or screenshots are required. Automatically graded result questions explicitly use the supplied reference results; live runs may differ. Use your own live results in the run-record short response, or identify supplied reference use. Core: 30 points. INF515 adds two BH questions worth 5 points, for 35 total. The preliminary automatic score is incomplete until the short responses are graded.

## Preparation

- Explain dN/dS and distinguish the three methods’ hypotheses.
- Interpret BUSTED’s gene-wide decision, aBSREL’s corrected branch p-values, and MEME’s uncorrected per-site threshold.
- Keep your three downloaded JSON files so you can reopen results later; result-page links are optional and are not graded. If a run fails, use the reference results and say so.
- Distinguish evidence of selection from evidence for its functional cause.

Use [MEME_codon_pvalues.csv](data/MEME_codon_pvalues.csv) and the README spreadsheet instructions. Apply BH across all 288 tests at FDR 0.05. Answer one automatically graded count question (2 points) and one short explanation (3 points). No extreme-omega or branch-restricted follow-up question is required. The BH demonstration video is optional support; the two extension questions are required for INF515.
